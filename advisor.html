Our Advisor

Mr. Prem Narayan Parashar

Mr. Prem Narayan Parashar is a highly respected legal professional with over 55 years of distinguished experience in the legal, judicial, tax and corporate sectors and is widely recognized as a legal luminary, scholar, writer, speaker, and consultant.

His legal journey began at Aligarh Muslim University, where he secured First Position and Gold Medals in both LL.B. and LL.M courses. Following his academic achievements, he served as a Lecturer in the Faculty of Law.

In 1972, Mr. Parashar secured the First Position in the Uttar Pradesh Judicial Services Examination, marking the beginning of an illustrious judicial and legal career.

Throughout his career, he has held several prestigious positions, including Judicial Member of ITAT, CAT, and Member of the Competition Commission of India.

He has also been associated with numerous academic, judicial, and professional institutions, contributing extensively to legal reforms, administration, and policy matters. Mr. Parashar has been actively practicing law for several decades, with expertise across diverse areas including matrimonial law, corporate matters, banking law, service matters, taxation, and civil litigation.

His practice extends before the Supreme Court of India, High Courts, Tribunals, and various judicial forums. As Founder and Senior Legal Consultant of Parashar & Parashar Law Firm, he has provided legal opinions, strategic advice, and consultancy services to prominent corporations, institutions, and organizations.

Mr. Parashar has delivered lectures and presentations at numerous national and international platforms on various legal subjects. His remarkable career stands as a testimony to dedication, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to justice and legal excellence.
